Liquid Glass, in parts

Each control sits on the kind of content it is meant to float over. The glass is always on the controls, never on the content: notes, photos and lists stay solid. Switch the appearance or turn on Reduce Transparency in the bar above to see the fallbacks.

Built with the apple-liquid-glass skill and its assets/tokens.css. Icons are simple stand-ins; SF Symbols are not licensed for web use.
